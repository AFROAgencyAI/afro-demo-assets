What Our Clients Say

Your Velvet Veil Glow-Up

  1. 1

    Microneedling with the Dermalogica PRO Pen

    Microneedling, also called collagen induction therapy, uses a medical-grade pen with very fine needles to make tiny, controlled channels in the skin. Your skin treats these channels as small injuries and repairs them by producing new collagen and elastin. The result is smoother, firmer, more even skin.

    The Dermalogica PRO Pen is FDA-cleared (510(k)) for improving the appearance of facial acne scars in adults 22 and older. It has adjustable needle depth, so the provider can fit the treatment to your skin.

  2. 2

    + FREE Dermalogica PRO Cooling Biocellulose Masque

    A professional sheet mask that goes on at the end of a treatment. It’s made of biocellulose, a material derived from coconut, coated in hyaluronic acid and soaked in a hydrating serum. Biocellulose clings to the face better than a regular paper sheet mask, so the serum spreads evenly and absorbs well.

    What it does for skin:

    • Leaves skin calm and hydrated, with a more comfortable first day afterward

    How long does it take to see results?

    Most clients notice an initial glow within 24–48 hours as skin begins to heal and regenerate. Deeper results (improved texture, tightness, and tone) typically appear within 2–4 weeks as collagen production kicks in.

    Results continue to improve over 3–6 months, with a series of 3 treatments delivering the most dramatic and lasting transformation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many treatments do I need?

Just one session is enough to see great results, but most clients do at least 3-6 treatments for maximum benefits.

Who cannot have this treatment?
  • Botox within the last 7 days
  • Filler within the last 14 days
  • Active acne, eczema, psoriasis
  • Stop using retinol or retinoids within 2 weeks of your appointment
  • No Accutane within the last year
  • No active sunburn
